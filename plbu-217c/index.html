Automated Process

Loan Application Intake & CRM Entry

0 min

Applications parsed automatically from all channels, deduplication check run, record created in Zoho CRM, and assigned to correct team queue based on loan type (personal, auto, renewable energy)

n8n + Zoho CRM webhook Saves 8 hrs/week

Complaints & Collections Case Logging

0 min

Inbound complaint emails and Amazon Connect call flags auto-trigger case creation in Zoho, categorised by type, assigned to the right agent, and acknowledgement email sent to customer automatically

Amazon Connect + Zoho + n8n Saves 6 hrs/week

Process Performance Reporting

0 min

Scheduled pipeline pulls data from Zoho and Databricks, compiles key metrics into a standardised Notion dashboard and Google Sheets report, distributed automatically to stakeholders every Monday morning

Databricks + Zapier + Notion Saves 6 hrs/week

Broker & Partner Onboarding

5 min (review only)

New partner form submission triggers Zoho record creation, automated welcome email sequence, document checklist via Notion, and access provisioning — with status tracked automatically until onboarding is complete

Zoho + n8n + Notion Saves 5 hrs/week

Workflow Bottleneck Detection & Alerts

0 min

Automated monitoring checks Zoho queues every hour for cases exceeding defined SLA thresholds; alerts sent directly to responsible staff via Slack with case details and suggested next action

Zoho + Zapier + Slack Saves 5 hrs/week

Stakeholder Requirements & Workshop Documentation

30 min (review & edit)

Workshop recordings automatically transcribed and summarised using an AI agent, with structured requirements drafted into a Notion template ready for BA review and sign-off — cutting documentation time by 75%

Claude API + Notion + Zapier Saves 4 hrs/week
