Current Process

Client Intake & Onboarding

45 min per new client

Done by: Care coordinators

Workflow Inefficiency Identification & Reporting

3 hrs per process review

Innovation leads manually interview department heads, compile notes, map processes in separate documents, and build reports to identify bottlenecks — repeated across every department initiative

Done by: Innovation & Transformation Leads

Care Plan & Compliance Documentation

2 hrs per care plan update

Staff manually draft, update, and version-control care plans and compliance documents, cross-referencing aged care regulatory requirements and emailing updated versions to stakeholders

Done by: Care coordinators, compliance staff

Provider & Supplier Invoice Processing

12 min per invoice

Finance and admin staff manually receive, check, and enter supplier and workforce provider invoices into the system, reconciling against service delivery records before approving payment

Done by: Finance and admin staff

Stakeholder Change Communication

1.5 hrs per communication round

Transformation leads manually draft update emails, meeting summaries, and training communications for each initiative rollout, tailoring messages for different departments and seniority levels

Done by: Innovation & Transformation Leads

Workforce Scheduling & Rostering Coordination

20 min per scheduling change

Coordinators manually match available home care workers to client appointments, handle shift change requests via phone and email, and notify workers of updates individually

Done by: Care coordinators

Automated Process

Client Intake & Onboarding

5 min review only

Digital intake form auto-populates CRM, triggers document checklist emails to client/family, and creates onboarding task list assigned to the relevant coordinator

Make + Airtable + DocuSign Saves 8 hrs/week

Workflow Inefficiency Identification & Reporting

30 min review and sign-off

AI agent analyses operational data, support tickets, and survey responses to surface inefficiency patterns and auto-generate a structured process review report for lead review

Claude API + n8n + Airtable Saves 6 hrs/week

Care Plan & Compliance Documentation

20 min review and approval

AI-assisted drafting pulls client data from CRM to pre-populate care plan templates, flags compliance gaps against current aged care standards, and routes documents for digital sign-off

Claude API + Make + DocuSign Saves 7 hrs/week

Provider & Supplier Invoice Processing

2 min exception handling

Invoices emailed to a dedicated inbox are parsed by AI, matched against service delivery records, and pushed into Xero for approval — exceptions flagged for manual review only

Make + Claude API + Xero Saves 5 hrs/week

Stakeholder Change Communication

15 min review and send

AI drafts tailored stakeholder communications from project update inputs, segmented by audience type, ready for lead review and one-click send via the team's email platform

Claude API + Power Automate + Outlook Saves 4 hrs/week

Workforce Scheduling & Rostering Coordination

3 min exception review

Scheduling rules engine auto-matches workers to client appointments based on availability, location, and care requirements; workers receive automated SMS/email notifications for shift updates

Make + Airtable + Twilio SMS Saves 4 hrs/week
