How Pacific Automation Can Save 34 Hours Per Week

Based on $80/hr average operational cost across sales, technical and admin staff × 48 working weeks

Current Process

RFQ & Technical Enquiry Triage

25 min per RFQ

Sales or technical staff manually read incoming RFQs and enquiries via email, decide priority, extract part numbers and specs, then type details into the CRM or ERP by hand

Done by: Sales / technical staff

Quote Preparation & Follow-Up

45 min per quote plus 10 min follow-up

Sales staff manually look up pricing in the ERP, check stock availability, write quote documents, email them to customers, then manually follow up by phone or email if no response after a few days

Done by: Sales staff

Technical Product Knowledge Retrieval

20 min per technical enquiry

Technical staff field repetitive product questions — hazardous area ratings, IECEx certifications, enclosure IP ratings, compatible accessories — by searching datasheets, emailing suppliers or relying on tribal knowledge from senior staff

Done by: Technical / sales staff

Purchase Order & Supplier Order Processing

20 min per purchase order

Purchasing staff manually check stock levels in the ERP, identify reorder triggers, draft purchase orders, email suppliers and then manually update the ERP when order confirmations arrive

Done by: Purchasing / admin staff

Customer Support Ticket Handling

15 min per ticket

Customer support staff manually read helpdesk tickets, look up order status in the ERP, check shipping tracking in the freight system, and compose individual replies — often pulling the same order data multiple times per day

Done by: Customer support staff

Compliance & Certification Document Management

30 min per compliance document request

Staff manually locate IECEx certificates, compliance declarations and product certifications when requested by customers or for audit purposes — searching shared drives, supplier portals and email threads, then compiling and sending document packs

Done by: Technical / admin staff

Sales & Operations Reporting

2.5 hrs per weekly report

Managers manually export data from the ERP and CRM into spreadsheets, build weekly and monthly reports on quote pipeline, order volumes, fulfilment performance and customer response times, then distribute via email

Done by: Sales manager / operations manager

Automated Process

RFQ & Technical Enquiry Triage

2 min (review only)

Inbound emails and web form submissions are parsed by an AI agent that extracts part numbers, quantities, site environment details and urgency signals, creates or updates the CRM record, tags enquiry type and routes to the correct rep or technical specialist

Claude API + Make + CRM webhook Saves 8 hrs/week

Quote Preparation & Follow-Up

10 min (review and send)

Quote is drafted automatically by pulling live pricing and stock from ERP via API, populated into a branded template and sent to the customer. A follow-up sequence triggers automatically at day 3 and day 7 if no response is logged in the CRM

Make + ERP API + CRM webhook Saves 7 hrs/week

Technical Product Knowledge Retrieval

2 min (confirm answer)

A structured knowledge base built from product datasheets, certification documents, application notes and captured expert knowledge is queried by an AI assistant. Staff and customers get accurate, sourced answers in under a minute without escalating to senior engineers

Claude API + vector knowledge base + internal chat integration Saves 6 hrs/week

Purchase Order & Supplier Order Processing

3 min (exception review)

Stock levels are monitored continuously via ERP API. When a reorder threshold is hit, a draft PO is generated and sent to the relevant supplier. Supplier confirmation emails are parsed and the ERP record is updated automatically, with exceptions flagged for human review

Make + ERP API + Claude API for email parsing Saves 5 hrs/week

Customer Support Ticket Handling

2 min (complex tickets only)

Incoming helpdesk tickets are classified by enquiry type. Order status and tracking queries are resolved automatically by pulling live data from the ERP and shipping system and sending a formatted reply. Complex or complaint tickets are escalated with full context pre-loaded for the agent

Make + ERP API + shipping API + helpdesk webhook Saves 4 hrs/week

Compliance & Certification Document Management

2 min (confirmation)

Certification and compliance documents are indexed against product SKUs in a structured repository. When a customer or auditor requests documentation, an automated workflow retrieves and packages the relevant certificates and sends them directly, with a log entry created for audit trail

Make + Airtable document index + Claude API Saves 2 hrs/week

Sales & Operations Reporting

10 min (review before distribution)

Scheduled automations pull live data from ERP and CRM, populate a standardised report template with key metrics — pipeline value, quote conversion, order fill rate, average response time — and distribute to relevant managers every Monday morning before business opens

Make + ERP API + CRM API + Google Sheets or Power BI Saves 2 hrs/week
