How Brisbane Festival Can Save 34 Hours Per Week

Based on $75/hr average operational cost × 48 working weeks

Current Process

Artist & Vendor Onboarding

25 min per contact

Admin staff manually email onboarding forms, chase responses, re-enter details from PDFs and emails into Airtable bases for each artist, performer, or supplier engaged across the festival

Done by: Admin staff

Scheduling & Production Coordination

3 hrs per schedule update

Production team manually compiles run sheets, schedules, and venue allocations across spreadsheets and emails, then distributes updates individually to each department when changes occur

Done by: Production & Admin staff

Asset & Equipment Tracking

20 min per asset transaction

Staff manually log equipment loans, set-up assets, and installation materials in spreadsheets, with no automated reminders for returns or status updates across multiple venues and suburban events

Done by: Production & Admin staff

Volunteer & Casual Staff Communications

2 hrs per communication round

Admin manually drafts and sends individual or bulk emails to volunteers and casual staff for shift confirmations, briefing documents, and schedule changes throughout the festival period

Done by: Admin staff

Sponsorship & Partnership Reporting

3 hrs per partner report

Development team manually compiles sponsorship deliverable reports from multiple sources including attendance data, media mentions, and asset usage logs, formatting and emailing reports to each partner individually

Done by: Development staff

Contractor Invoice & Document Collection

15 min per contractor per follow-up

Admin staff chase contractors individually via email for invoices, tax declarations, and compliance documents, manually tracking what has been received and following up outstanding items before processing

Done by: Admin staff

Automated Process

Artist & Vendor Onboarding

0 min

Fillout form auto-triggers on contract sign-off, responses populate Airtable base directly, contact record created, welcome email sent, and relevant department notified automatically

Airtable + Fillout + Make Saves 6 hrs/week

Scheduling & Production Coordination

5 min review only

Schedule changes in Airtable trigger automated notifications to relevant departments via email or Slack, updated run sheets auto-generated via Docupilot and distributed to stakeholders

Airtable Automations + Docupilot + Make Saves 7 hrs/week

Asset & Equipment Tracking

2 min per transaction

Asset check-in/check-out logged via Airtable form or miniExtensions interface, automated reminders sent via Twilio SMS when return dates approach, status dashboard updated in real time

Airtable + miniExtensions + Twilio Saves 5 hrs/week

Volunteer & Casual Staff Communications

10 min setup per campaign

Shift assignments in Airtable trigger personalised confirmation emails via SendGrid, briefing documents attached automatically via Docupilot, with opt-out and response tracking captured back into the base

Airtable + SendGrid + Docupilot Saves 6 hrs/week

Sponsorship & Partnership Reporting

15 min review only

Airtable aggregates deliverable data across bases, Docupilot auto-generates branded partner reports on a scheduled trigger, reports emailed to partners automatically via SendGrid with a tracked link

Airtable + Docupilot + SendGrid + Make Saves 5 hrs/week

Contractor Invoice & Document Collection

0 min

Automated follow-up sequence triggered when contractor record is created in Airtable, Fillout form collects documents and tax details, status field updated automatically on receipt, finance team notified when complete

Airtable + Fillout + Make + Zapier Saves 5 hrs/week
