How SeenCulture Can Save 29 Hours Per Week

Based on $75/hr average operational cost × 29 hrs/week × 48 working weeks

Current Process

Customer Pipeline Handoff & CRM Entry

20 min per lead

Founder or team member manually transfers inbound leads and demo requests from email/Slack into CRM, assigns follow-up tasks, and logs conversation context by hand

Done by: Founder / Ops

Proposal & Contract Generation

45 min per proposal

Ops or founder manually drafts proposals and contracts for each new customer by copying from previous versions, updating names, scope, and pricing, then formatting for send

Done by: Founder / Ops

Customer Onboarding Workflow

2 hrs per new account

Each new customer onboarding is managed manually — welcome emails written from scratch, setup steps tracked in Slack or memory, platform access granted ad hoc, and progress not systematically recorded

Done by: Ops / Founder

Internal Decision & Context Capture

3 hrs per week reconstructing context

Decisions made in Slack, Zoom calls, or ad hoc conversations are not systematically recorded. Context is lost when revisiting past choices, causing repeated discussions and lost institutional knowledge.

Done by: Founder / Ops / Whole team

Product Analytics & CRM Unified Reporting

2.5 hrs per weekly report

Product usage data and CRM pipeline data sit in separate tools. Compiling a weekly view of customer health, engagement, and pipeline status requires manual export, spreadsheet merging, and formatting.

Done by: Ops / Founder

UAT Coordination & Release Feedback Loops

3 hrs per release cycle

User acceptance testing feedback collected via email or Slack threads, manually collated into a document, and summarised for the product lead. Follow-ups tracked informally with no consistent structure.

Done by: Ops / Simon (fractional Growth & Product)

Finance Ops & Vendor/Subscription Management

3 hrs per month on reconciliation and tracking

Invoices from vendors, SaaS subscriptions, and contractor payments tracked manually in a spreadsheet. Receipts collected ad hoc, reconciliation done at end of month, often requiring backtracking through email and Slack.

Done by: Founder / Ops

Automated Process

Customer Pipeline Handoff & CRM Entry

0 min

Inbound leads from web forms, LinkedIn, and email auto-parsed and created as CRM contacts with source, company size, and notes populated. Follow-up task auto-assigned to relevant owner.

Make + HubSpot webhook + Claude API Saves 5 hrs/week

Proposal & Contract Generation

2 min review

CRM deal data triggers auto-generation of a branded proposal and contract using pre-approved templates. Document sent via e-signature tool with tracking. Signed copy logged back to CRM automatically.

Make + Claude API + PandaDoc Saves 5 hrs/week

Customer Onboarding Workflow

15 min oversight

Contract signed triggers automated onboarding sequence: welcome email sent, platform access provisioned, onboarding checklist created in project tracker, and check-in reminders scheduled. Progress tracked without manual input.

Make + Airtable + HubSpot Saves 6 hrs/week

Internal Decision & Context Capture

10 min review per week

Meeting transcripts auto-processed by Claude to extract decisions, owners, and rationale. Summaries posted to a searchable Notion decision log. Slack messages flagged with a custom emoji trigger an automated capture workflow.

Claude API + Make + Notion Saves 3 hrs/week

Product Analytics & CRM Unified Reporting

0 min

Automated pipeline pulls product analytics and CRM data into a unified Airtable dashboard. Weekly summary report auto-generated and delivered to founders every Monday morning with engagement flags and at-risk account alerts.

Make + Airtable + Claude API Saves 5 hrs/week

UAT Coordination & Release Feedback Loops

20 min review

UAT participants receive structured feedback forms triggered by release milestones. Responses auto-aggregated and Claude summarises themes, flags blockers, and formats a release-ready brief delivered to Simon and the founder.

Make + Typeform + Claude API + Notion Saves 3 hrs/week

Finance Ops & Vendor/Subscription Management

20 min monthly review

Incoming invoices auto-captured from email, parsed by Claude for vendor, amount, and due date, then pushed to Xero for coding and approval. Subscription renewals tracked in Airtable with automated renewal alerts 14 days in advance.

Make + Claude API + Xero + Airtable Saves 2 hrs/week
