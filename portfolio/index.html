Operations dashboard · Production web app

Recovered ~500 hours a year.

  • What it is. A production dashboard for a three-city trade business: builder check-ins, contractor profiles, four sales queues and gross profit in one place.
  • Why it exists. Managers were rebuilding the week by hand from Tradify, Xero, Notion and Sheets, and the numbers rarely agreed.
  • What it does now. Nightly sync from the tools the team already uses, each city walled off in the database, in daily use by three managers and the CEO.
  • Result. About 10 hours a week of manager time back, measured at project close.
Automation strategy · Consulting

Found $59K a year in hidden work.

  • What it is. A strategy report mapping 21 automations across operations, hiring, finance, property and marketing.
  • Why it exists. The founder had a list of 13 manual tasks and no way to know which were worth automating first.
  • How it works. A manual-hours baseline by function, then every automation scored on impact, return and ease and sequenced into five phases.
  • Result. 140+ hours a month of manual work, about $59K a year at an Australian admin rate, with the shared engine three later builds depend on scheduled first.

Speed to lead · CRM and AI

Up to $360K a year in new revenue.

  • What it is. A lead-response system on HubSpot, WhatsApp and JustCall for a fitness education provider with four sales reps.
  • Why it exists. Every lead sat in one manual queue. The median first response was 5.4 days, and the leads that enrol waited behind the ones that never would.
  • How it works. Routing by source and country at creation, an automated first touch inside five minutes, after-hours AI response, and a same-day human rule for the 24% of leads that produce 85% of enrolments.
  • Result. AUD 15k to 30k a month in additional enrolments, AUD 180k to 360k a year, from response time alone.
