How BX Bunka Australia Can Save 34 Hours Per Week

Based on $75/hr average operational cost × 34 hrs/week × 48 working weeks

Current Process

Customer Enquiry & Lead Capture

15 min per enquiry, ~30 enquiries/week

Admin staff manually transcribe customer enquiries from phone calls, emails, and web forms into spreadsheets, then forward to the appropriate sales or service team member

Done by: Admin staff

Quote Preparation & Approval

45 min per quote, ~15 quotes/week

Sales reps manually compile product specs, pricing, and customer details into quote documents using Word or Excel templates, then email for internal approval before sending to the customer

Done by: Sales reps & sales manager

Service & Maintenance Job Scheduling

20 min per job scheduling, ~25 jobs/week

Service coordinator manually matches incoming maintenance requests to available technicians, checks locations, and updates a shared calendar by phone and email back-and-forth

Done by: Service coordinator

Installation & Service Job Reporting

25 min per report (admin entry), ~20 reports/week

Technicians complete paper-based job completion forms on-site, which are then collected, manually keyed into the system by admin staff, and filed for compliance and warranty records

Done by: Technicians & admin staff

Invoice Generation & Accounts Payable

12 min per invoice, ~30 invoices/week

Admin staff manually generate invoices from completed job records, cross-check against purchase orders, and enter data into the accounting system before sending to customers

Done by: Finance / admin staff

Product & Parts Inventory Reordering

2 hrs per week manual stock review and PO creation

Warehouse staff manually check stock levels of door components and parts, compile a reorder list, and email purchase requests to procurement for approval and supplier ordering

Done by: Warehouse staff & procurement

Automated Process

Customer Enquiry & Lead Capture

0 min

Enquiries from web forms, email, and phone callback requests are auto-parsed, a lead record created in the CRM, tagged by product type (residential, commercial, industrial), and assigned to the correct sales rep with an automated acknowledgement email sent to the customer

Power Automate + Dynamics 365 CRM Saves 7.5 hrs/week

Quote Preparation & Approval

8 min (input only)

Rep selects product type and inputs dimensions; automation pulls current pricing, generates a branded PDF quote, routes it for manager e-approval via Teams, and emails the approved quote to the customer — all tracked in the CRM

Power Automate + Power Apps + SharePoint Saves 9.25 hrs/week

Service & Maintenance Job Scheduling

2 min (review only)

Maintenance requests trigger automated technician availability check, geo-proximity matching, and calendar booking. Technician receives job details via mobile app; customer receives confirmation SMS with appointment time

Power Automate + SimPRO + Power Apps Saves 7.5 hrs/week

Installation & Service Job Reporting

0 min (admin entry eliminated)

Technicians complete a mobile digital form on-site with photos attached; submission auto-creates the job completion record, updates the service history in the CRM, triggers customer satisfaction survey, and files the report in SharePoint

Power Apps mobile + Power Automate + SharePoint Saves 8.0 hrs/week

Invoice Generation & Accounts Payable

2 min (exceptions only)

On job completion sign-off, invoice is auto-generated from the job record, matched against the purchase order, pushed to Xero, and emailed to the customer with a payment link — exceptions flagged for human review only

Power Automate + Xero API Saves 5.0 hrs/week

Product & Parts Inventory Reordering

15 min (approval only)

Stock levels monitored in real time; when thresholds are breached, a draft purchase order is auto-generated, routed for approval via Teams, and submitted to the supplier on approval — with delivery tracked in the system

Power Automate + Power Apps + ERP integration Saves 1.75 hrs/week
