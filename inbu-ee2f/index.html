How Innovior Can Save 34 Hours Per Week

Based on $75/hr average operational cost × 48 working weeks

Current Process

Client Enquiry & Lead Intake

20 min per enquiry

Consultant manually reviews inbound client enquiries via email, logs contact details and project scope into a spreadsheet, and routes to the appropriate team member

Done by: Business Consultant / Admin

Client Proposal & Scope Estimation

3–4 hrs per proposal

Consultant manually assembles proposal documents from scratch, pulling service descriptions, prior case studies, and effort estimates from various folders and spreadsheets

Done by: Business Consultant

Requirements & Workshop Documentation

2–3 hrs per workshop

After client workshops, consultant manually transcribes notes, formats requirements into user stories and process maps, and distributes to the project team

Done by: Business Consultant

Project Status Reporting

1.5–2 hrs per report per project

Consultant manually compiles weekly status updates by pulling task progress from multiple tools, writing commentary, and emailing formatted reports to client stakeholders

Done by: Business Consultant

Timesheet & Resource Logging

30–45 min per person per week

Consultants manually enter daily time logs into a spreadsheet or billing system, often done in batches at end of week leading to errors and delays in project cost tracking

Done by: All Consultants

Onboarding New Clients

2–3 hrs per new client

Admin and consultant manually send welcome emails, collect signed NDAs and contracts, set up project folders, and create accounts across platforms one step at a time

Done by: Business Consultant / Admin

Invoice Generation & Follow-Up

45 min per invoice cycle

Admin manually creates invoices from timesheet summaries and milestone completions, sends to clients, then chases overdue payments via individual follow-up emails

Done by: Admin / Finance

Automated Process

Client Enquiry & Lead Intake

0 min

Inbound enquiries parsed from email or web form, lead record auto-created in CRM, tagged by industry vertical, and assigned to relevant consultant with Slack notification

Make + HubSpot CRM Saves 5 hrs/week

Client Proposal & Scope Estimation

30 min (review only)

Proposal template auto-populated with client details, relevant service modules, and pre-approved case studies based on industry tag; delivered as a draft for consultant review

Make + Claude API + Google Docs Saves 6 hrs/week

Requirements & Workshop Documentation

20 min (review only)

Workshop transcript auto-processed to extract requirements, generate draft user stories, and produce a structured summary document shared to the project workspace

n8n + Claude API + Confluence Saves 7 hrs/week

Project Status Reporting

15 min (review only)

Status report auto-generated by pulling task completions, blockers, and milestones from project tracker; formatted and emailed to client contacts on a scheduled cadence

Make + Monday.com + Gmail Saves 6 hrs/week

Timesheet & Resource Logging

5 min (approval only)

Calendar events and task completions automatically mapped to timesheet entries in the billing system; consultant receives a pre-filled weekly summary to approve in one click

Zapier + Google Calendar + Harvest Saves 4 hrs/week

Onboarding New Clients

10 min (confirmation check)

Triggered by CRM deal closure: welcome email sent, e-signature request issued, project folder created, and team workspace provisioned automatically with all standard templates

Make + DocuSign + Google Drive + Slack Saves 4 hrs/week

Invoice Generation & Follow-Up

5 min (review only)

Invoices auto-generated from approved timesheets and milestone triggers, sent via Xero, with automated payment reminder sequences triggered at 7, 14, and 30 days overdue

Make + Xero Saves 2 hrs/week
